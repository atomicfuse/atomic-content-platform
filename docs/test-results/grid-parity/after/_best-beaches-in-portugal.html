Best Beaches in Portugal

By Editorial Team · September 1, 2026

Portugal's coastline stretches from the wild Atlantic cliffs of the Algarve to the golden sands near Lisbon, and picking a single "best" beach is nearly impossible — but Praia da Marinha, Praia da Falésia, and Praia do Camilo consistently top the list for their turquoise water, dramatic rock formations, and easy access from nearby towns.
